About Quantam Core

Quantam Core is an emerging game development and mobile application company based in Bongaon, West Bengal, India. Founded on September 17, 2025, the company is driven by a passion for innovation, creativity, and technology. With a strong focus on delivering engaging digital experiences, Quantam Core specializes in developing high-quality games using the Unity engine, alongside modern mobile applications built with advanced development tools such as Android Studio and Google Antigravity.

The company was established with the ambition to create impactful and widely recognized digital products that resonate with global audiences. By combining technical expertise with creative storytelling, Quantam Core aims to push the boundaries of interactive entertainment. The team is dedicated to continuous learning, experimentation, and improvement, ensuring that every project reflects both quality and originality.

Our Vision

The vision of Quantam Core is to become a globally recognized name in the gaming and application development industry. The company strives to create innovative, immersive, and high-performance digital experiences that not only entertain but also inspire users worldwide.
Quantam Core envisions building a strong portfolio of successful games and applications that stand out in competitive markets, while also establishing a sustainable and scalable business model. Through dedication, creativity, and technological excellence, the goal is to transform ideas into powerful digital realities and contribute meaningfully to the future of interactive media.
