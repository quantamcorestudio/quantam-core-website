About Me

Bridging technology, game development, entrepreneurship, and pharmaceutical sciences.

Hello! I am Pradipta Pal (pronounced "Prodipto Pal"), a multi-disciplinary tech enthusiast and entrepreneur currently pursuing a Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) at Vidyasagar Pharmaceutical College. My foundational science and computer journey began during my schooling at Bongaon High School.

I established my first business, Blue Whale Store, serving digital gaming merchandise and tech products. Driven by a passion for game engineering, I co-founded Quantum Core Gaming Studio alongside my esteemed team members Tanay Halder and Subhrojyoti Roy.
